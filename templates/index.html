How a collaboration works

  1. Sign up and get checked

    Sign in with Google and tell us about yourself. We verify your profile and social accounts before anyone can work with you.

  2. Get matched

    Brands post a campaign. We suggest the creators and event partners whose audience fits it.

  3. Agree in writing

    Both sides sign the brief, the deliverables and the dates before any work starts.

  4. Deliver and approve

    The creator submits the work. The brand approves it or asks for changes, and OPC steps in if there’s a dispute.
